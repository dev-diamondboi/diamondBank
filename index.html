Is Diamond Bank a real bank?

Diamond Bank is a fictional brand and a working portfolio project. All balances, transfers, payments, and cards are simulated. It does not hold deposits or move real money.

What can I do in the demo?

Create an account, explore your dashboard, move demo funds, make simulated payments, build savings goals, and try card controls. Your changes are saved, so you can return to them later.

Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. Every new demo account starts with sample funds. You never need to provide real bank details or payment information.

Can I start fresh?

Yes. Choose Reset demo in Settings to restore the starting balances and goals. Your profile and sign-in details stay the same.
